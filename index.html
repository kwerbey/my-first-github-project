Hello! My name is Kirby Wayne Naelgas, and I am an 18-year-old second-year Information Technology student at National University Fairview. I consider myself a curious, motivated, and ambitious individual who enjoys exploring new opportunities, gaining knowledge, and continuously improving myself. Being one of the youngest in my circle has encouraged me to become more independent, open-minded, and eager to learn from the experiences of others.

Outside academics, I have a strong interest in entrepreneurship, particularly in building and managing businesses, handling finances, and understanding how to make practical and strategic decisions.
